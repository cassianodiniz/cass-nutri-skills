## Figura 1 - Key elements of nutritional priorities to support GLP-1 therapy for obesity

O diagrama é um círculo com oito setores ao redor do tema central "Nutritional Priorities to Support GLP-1 Therapy for Obesity". Os setores representam prioridades complementares; as posições não indicam sequência temporal, proporções nutricionais, doses ou intensidade relativa.

1. Initiation of GLP-1 therapy with a patient-centered approach.
2. Completion of baseline nutritional assessment and screening.
3. Management of gastrointestinal side effects.
4. Navigation of dietary preferences and intakes.
5. Prevention and mitigation of nutrient deficiencies.
6. Preservation of muscle and bone mass.
7. Maximization of weight reduction efficacy.
8. Promotion of other supportive lifestyle measures.

## Tabela 4 - The 5As framework applied to support nutrition and lifestyle for obesity care using GLP-1 therapy

| Step | Key components | Examples of topics to address |
|---|---|---|
| Assess | Life stage; medical history and diagnoses; physical exam, laboratory tests; food and nutrition security, dietary history and assessment; social determinants of health; psychosocial factors, including mental stress and cultural and familial food preferences; other potential barriers to change, such as food allergies or intolerances. | Age of onset of problems with excess weight; periods of rapid weight gain and triggers; previously attempted weight interventions, including formal or informal diet/lifestyle programs, meal replacement, medically monitored programs, very low-calorie diets, medications, weight reduction supplements, and metabolic procedures including devices and surgeries. |
| Advise | Benefits and risks of GLP-1 assessment; essential complementary role of long-term nutrition and lifestyle change; nutrition and lifestyle as foundations of health, with benefits beyond weight alone, with GLP-1 as adjunctive therapy (a re-setting of the drug-focused medical paradigm). | Nutritional and physical activity recommendations; GLP-1 side effects; compliance with dosing schedule. |
| Agree | Shared plan of care so that weight reduction and general health goals are understood and expectations are appropriate. | Shared decisions on target weight and continuity of care, including follow-up appointments; culturally tailored meal and exercise plans; S.M.A.R.T. prescriptions (Specific, Measurable, Actionable, Realistic, Time-sensitive) for eating and activity goals. |
| Assist | Address challenges and barriers such as food access, transportation, and need for financial resources. | Eligibility assessment and enrollment support for food assistance such as SNAP, if eligible; help finding local physical activity resources such as parks and recreation centers. |
| Arrange | Refer as needed to other specialists. | Registered dietitian nutritionist; behavioral therapist; social worker; case manager. |

Nota 1: o modelo pode ser adaptado para começar por **Ask**, pedindo permissão para conversar sobre peso e hábitos alimentares. A nota não substitui nenhum dos cinco passos apresentados na tabela.
